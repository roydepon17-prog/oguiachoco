Terroir & Provenance

Nurtured Beneath the Canopy of Western Visayas

At DAD’s Farm in Maayon, Capiz, cacao is more than a harvest—it is an expression of place. Our trees flourish beneath a diverse agroforestry canopy of native fruit trees, coconut palms, and tropical flora. The soil, climate, biodiversity, cacao genetics, and careful post-harvest craft come together to shape the distinctive character of our beans. From the land of Capiz to every bar, O’GUIA captures a taste of place—Philippine cacao expressed through the landscape, heritage, and hands of Panay.

By eliminating middlemen, O'Guia Chocolates directly empowers local farming families through dignified fair living wages, generational agricultural education, and sustainable regenerative agroforestry that guarantees the future of Philippine craft chocolate.

Frequently Inquired

Traceability, national dispatch logistics, and optimal storage parameters.

How is chocolate dispatched nationwide without melting? expand_more

All provincial orders (Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao, and Luzon) are packaged within insulated thermal wrappers with chilled gel packs and dispatched via air-express priority courier. We ship Monday through Wednesday to avoid weekend warehouse transit halts.

What is the recommended storage temperature for O'Guia bars? expand_more

To preserve the delicate crystalline cocoa butter structure, store your bars between 18°C and 20°C in a dry, odorless setting. If refrigerating in tropical warmth, keep tightly sealed in airtight wrapping and let rest at room temperature for 15 minutes before tasting.

Is the Keto Dark Chocolate truly zero-sugar and diabetic-friendly? expand_more

Yes. Our Keto bar contains 0g refined cane sugar, relying exclusively on premium non-GMO erythritol and high natural fiber content from Maayon cacao nibs, ensuring an ultra-low glycemic response with zero insulin surge.

How do I prepare the pure O'Guia Tablea for Rolled Oats Champorado? expand_more

Boil 2 cups of fresh water with 2-3 rounds of O'Guia Tablea until fully dissolved and aromatic. Whisk vigorously, stir in 1 cup of whole rolled oats, and simmer for 8 minutes. Finish with coconut nectar or muscovado, drizzled with evaporada or almond milk.

What are lead times for bespoke corporate gifting and weddings? expand_more

Custom branded sleeve bands, bespoke bonbon flights, and harvest gift crates require 2 to 3 weeks advance allocation depending on micro-batch cellar availability and batch conching timelines.
